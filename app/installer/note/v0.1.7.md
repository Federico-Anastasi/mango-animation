Windows 10 or 11, 64-bit only.

**Install:** download the installer below (`Mango.Animation_0.1.7_x64-setup.exe`) and run it. If you have an earlier version, the app offers this one by itself at the next start; it installs over it and keeps your settings and your work.

**What you need:** one chat engine, installed and signed in: Claude Code (a Claude account on a subscription that includes it: Pro, Max, Ultra, Team or Enterprise; the free plan does not), Codex (your ChatGPT account) or Mango Code (runs on your computer, no sign-in). One is enough. If none is ready, the first start lists them with an "Install" or "Sign in" button for each.

**Changed since 0.1.6**
- The chat works with three engines: Claude Code, Codex and Mango Code. In a new conversation the Model menu lists the models of every engine that is ready, and the one you pick decides the engine; a conversation keeps the engine it started with. Method, skills and tools are the same with all three.
- A new look for the whole app: the start page, the top bar, every page of the studio, the chat and the phone pages share the same colours, cards, switches and icons.
- Settings are one page: a column with Search, General and Help on the left, the settings in cards on the right. General has the size of the interface; Help has the version, "Check now" for updates, the logs and "Report a problem".
- The start page shows the start in steps, one piece of the bar for each, and the mango moves while the app is starting.
- While the chat waits for a long job (an edit, a render), a message you write is read at once, not when the job ends (Claude Code and Codex).
- A step of the chat that you stopped says "Stopped before it finished", not that it succeeded.
- "Restart and update" is off while a job is running or waiting, and says why.
- With the chat open the page keeps at least 420 px, also after you make the window smaller, and Settings arrange themselves on the width that is left.
- Narrow windows and the phone pages: everything you touch is at least 44 px high.
- Where you can pick more than one answer or tick an item, the chat shows checkboxes.

**What to expect**
- The installer is not signed with a code-signing certificate: Windows SmartScreen may ask you to confirm ("More info" → "Run anyway").
- "Precise cuts on words" uses the MMS model, licensed for non-commercial use only: if you earn from your videos, turn it off at first start or later in Settings › Computer.

Please report problems with "Report a problem" in the app, or in the Issues with what you did and what happened.
