Windows 10 or 11, 64-bit only.

**Install:** download the installer below (`Mango.Animation_0.1.3_x64-setup.exe`) and run it. If you have an earlier version, the app offers this one by itself at the next start; it installs over it and keeps your settings and your work.

**What you need:** Claude Code with a Claude account on a subscription that includes it (Pro, Max, Ultra, Team or Enterprise; the free plan does not). If Claude Code is missing, the app installs it with one click and then asks you to sign in.

**Changed since 0.1.2**
- Videos from your computer: "Add video" in Footage, or drop the files on the page, with progress and the reason for each file that does not get in.
- Every video shows the eight steps of the method: where you are and what to say in the chat to move on.
- Edit: "Versions" of the plan, with date and time, "Look" and "Go back to this one". Your changes and Claude's are saved one gesture at a time and no longer overwrite each other. A cut made by hand snaps to the words as the edit counts them.
- Footage: "Search the speech" looks in every file already transcribed and opens the file at that second.
- A Settings page (Profile, Style, Computer, Phone, General) and a Channel entry (Audience, Ranking, Growth) that appears once a channel has been read. In the bar, "Phone" and "Jobs" show up only when there is something to say.
- Style: three sample cards to choose a style by looking at it, a choice of font and "Add a font".
- English: the chat's messages and errors and the pages you open from your phone are now in both languages.
- Office networks: downloads, reports and the update check use the proxy and the certificates of Windows, and say in plain words when a company filter stops them.
- Cutout: it no longer takes in what is above your face (a lamp behind your head), it starts from the first frame where you are visible, and it no longer grabs the whole room for a few frames.
- Subtitles on light backgrounds have a thinner outline, so the letters stay readable.
- Reading TikTok works inside the app, and says so in one sentence when Chrome is closed.
- Chat: Claude's steps in a row fold into one line you can open, the model menu shows the most recent models first, and a long conversation tells you when a new one is the better choice.
- "Report a problem" no longer collects your email or your Claude plan.
- The app starts without waiting more than 5 seconds for the update check, and removes old installers left in the temporary folder.
- Many fixes: files confirmed from the phone are no longer deleted after 24 hours, an apostrophe in the folder's path no longer breaks an edit, a page that breaks shows a sentence with "Reload" instead of a blank screen, and moving the data folder is safer if it stops halfway.

**What to expect**
- The installer is not signed with a code-signing certificate: Windows SmartScreen may ask you to confirm ("More info" → "Run anyway").
- "Precise cuts on words" uses the MMS model, licensed for non-commercial use only: if you earn from your videos, turn it off at first start or later in Settings › Computer.

Please report problems with "Report a problem" in the app, or in the Issues with what you did and what happened.
