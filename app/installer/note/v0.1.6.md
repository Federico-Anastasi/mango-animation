Windows 10 or 11, 64-bit only.

**Install:** download the installer below (`Mango.Animation_0.1.6_x64-setup.exe`) and run it. If you have an earlier version, the app offers this one by itself at the next start; it installs over it and keeps your settings and your work.

**What you need:** Claude Code with a Claude account on a subscription that includes it (Pro, Max, Ultra, Team or Enterprise; the free plan does not). If Claude Code is missing, the app installs it with one click and then asks you to sign in.

**Changed since 0.1.5**
- Settings › Style: "Start from a style" works again. Version 0.1.5 was installed without the starting styles and showed an error there and in the first round.
- After an update the app no longer asks to download all of Python again (5 GB, and "not enough space" on a small disk) when only one small package is new: it counts what is really missing.
- One width for every page: Channel and Settings no longer jump sideways when you change tab, tables and charts use the whole page, forms stay on the left, and the top bar lines up with the page.
- The first round starts with Claude's greeting, not with a message that looks written by you, and Claude's steps stay folded in one line with a plain sentence about what it is doing.
- Channel › Audience: the videos you published show their thumbnail, title and comments also when the last reading of the channel did not link them to your archive.
- English: the lines and the errors of the animations, of the tools and of a failed download now follow the language of the studio.
- A profile with something wrong says what to fix in plain words, in both languages, instead of technical names.
- The chat has a step of its own for writing the script of a video.

**What to expect**
- The installer is not signed with a code-signing certificate: Windows SmartScreen may ask you to confirm ("More info" → "Run anyway").
- "Precise cuts on words" uses the MMS model, licensed for non-commercial use only: if you earn from your videos, turn it off at first start or later in Settings › Computer.

Please report problems with "Report a problem" in the app, or in the Issues with what you did and what happened.
