Windows 10 or 11, 64-bit only.

**Install:** download the installer below (`Mango.Animation_0.1.8_x64-setup.exe`) and run it. If you have an earlier version, the app offers this one by itself at the next start; it installs over it and keeps your settings and your work.

**What you need:** one chat engine, installed and signed in: Claude Code (a Claude account on a subscription that includes it: Pro, Max, Ultra, Team or Enterprise; the free plan does not), Codex (your ChatGPT account) or Mango Code (runs on your computer, no sign-in). One is enough. If none is ready, the first start lists them with an "Install" or "Sign in" button for each.

**Changed since 0.1.7**
- The size of the interface no longer grows by itself at every start on computers where the Windows text size is not 100%. If yours grew, set it back once in Settings › General.
- The first round no longer starts by itself: the app says which engine and model the chat is about to start with, and waits for "Start". A Claude Code conversation you reopen goes on with the model it was using.
- "Report a problem": you can attach an image you saved, and "Include a screenshot of the window" starts off everywhere.
- The update notice only says that a new version is out.
- Uninstalling removes the whole app folder, its packages included (about 520 MB). Your data stays.
- Videos: inside each status the cards are in date order, newest first: published videos by the day they came out, the others by the day they were created.
- Transcription picks by itself a computation your video card can do, so older NVIDIA cards work without editing the profile.
- A take with a voice but no words heard gives a clear message instead of an error nobody can read.
- Problems in a plan or a card are said in plain words, and the error of a job arrives whole, not just its last line.
- Photos from the phone have their own type, "Photo".
- With the chat open on very wide windows the top bar lines up with the page; on a phone the ranking no longer runs off the screen.

**What to expect**
- The installer is not signed with a code-signing certificate: Windows SmartScreen may ask you to confirm ("More info" → "Run anyway").
- "Precise cuts on words" uses the MMS model, licensed for non-commercial use only: if you earn from your videos, turn it off at first start or later in Settings › Computer.

Please report problems with "Report a problem" in the app, or in the Issues with what you did and what happened.
