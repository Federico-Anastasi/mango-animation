Windows 10 or 11, 64-bit only.

**Install:** download the installer below (`Mango.Animation_0.1.2_x64-setup.exe`) and run it. If you have 0.1.0 or 0.1.1, the app offers this version by itself at the next start; it installs over it and keeps your settings and your work.

**What you need:** Claude Code with a Claude account on a subscription that includes it (Pro, Max, Ultra, Team or Enterprise; the free plan does not). If Claude Code is missing, the app installs it with one click and then asks you to sign in.

**Changed since 0.1.1**
- A first start that guides you: Claude Code installed and signed in, then where to keep your videos, with the free space of each disk and a warning for folders synced by OneDrive.
- A first conversation with Claude: your name and language, who you make videos for and where you publish (TikTok, Instagram, YouTube, X), what is never said, and your style. If you already publish videos, Claude can look at them, with your permission, and start your style from them; otherwise you pick a starting style or describe it. Then it shows you around the app.
- Instagram is one of the channels, with vertical videos for Reels.
- A neutral default style, which yours sits on top of.
- Pages that need data from your channels explain what they do and how to get started while they are empty.
- "Move the data folder…" now moves your files, with progress and cancel.
- Dictation and transcription work on NVIDIA cards without a separate CUDA install; if a library is missing, the app says so when it loads instead of failing on your first sentence.
- Zoom the window with Ctrl+ and Ctrl- (Ctrl+0 goes back to 100%); the zoom is remembered.

**What to expect**
- The installer is not signed with a code-signing certificate: Windows SmartScreen may ask you to confirm ("More info" → "Run anyway").
- "Precise cuts on words" uses the MMS model, licensed for non-commercial use only: if you earn from your videos, turn it off at first start or later on the Engines page.

Please report problems with "Report a problem" in the app, or in the Issues with what you did and what happened.
