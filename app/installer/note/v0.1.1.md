Windows 10 or 11, 64-bit only.

**Install:** download the installer below (`Mango.Animation_0.1.1_x64-setup.exe`) and run it. If you have 0.1.0, the app offers this version by itself at the next start; it installs over it and keeps your settings and your work.

**Changed since 0.1.0**
- Videos without an audio track work: a silent recording can be cut by time (`da` and `a`) and edited with silence, and a piece without voice can show its silent footage (`durata` with `girato`), for example under a voice recorded later. Where a step needs the voice, the message says that the file has no audio.
- The studio says when a file has no audio, instead of saying that its speech has not been transcribed yet.
- Links and pages opened by the app, and the studio opened in the browser, stay open when the app quits.
- A job that Claude sends to the background shows the tokens and tools it has used so far.
- During the first setup, the bar of Python and its packages moves while they download.
- On the Style page, frame labels that fall in the same place no longer overlap.
- Claude answers in a more direct and concise way.

**What to expect**
- The installer is not signed with a code-signing certificate: Windows SmartScreen may ask you to confirm ("More info" → "Run anyway").
- "Precise cuts on words" uses the MMS model, licensed for non-commercial use only: if you earn from your videos, turn it off at first start or later on the Engines page.

Please report problems with "Report a problem" in the app, or in the Issues with what you did and what happened.
