# Content

What is said in a video and how it is structured. These are the product's defaults: the creator changes them in the profile
(`.claude/rules/` of the working folder, `profilo.json`), and the profile wins.

## Structure

- **Always open with a hook**, never with the explanation: a tension, not an announcement.
- **Three movements** (default structure): the viewer's problem, the creator's position, the practical solutions. The material is not
  divided by topic nor by how the research was done.
- **Synthesis**: linear, simple, direct speech. One case, one perimeter, the conclusions on that perimeter. The work done is said in
  one sentence, not told.
- **The level is the viewer's** (`pubblico.livello`): for a non-technical viewer, no acronyms, no technical numbers on screen, no
  code or commands, no money figures unless the discourse needs them.
- **The close** is a precise question or a stand. The question asks for the viewer's experience («have you tried it? how did it go?»),
  and promises nothing in exchange for a comment. The platforms' notes (`tiktok.md`, `youtube.md`) declare their exceptions.
- **More videos from the same source** each have a different hook and a different center; the few seconds of «what we are doing» may
  resemble each other, but are each time taken from a different point.

## What is stated

- Before proposing a video and after editing it, reread what is said and shown looking for errors: a wrong name, a number that
  contradicts the screen or our data, something said aloud that is not so. What does not add up is flagged to the creator
  (`da_verificare` in the card) with a proposed remedy: cut the sentence, do not frame that point, correct the writing.
- Numbers on screen in the animations come from the creator's own sources (`fonti_delle_idee` in the profile) and measurements, with,
  next to them in the scene's data file, where they come from.
- This is not a blocking check: what the creator says in the video stays theirs.

## What is not said

What the creator does not say is in the profile (`non_si_dice`, and their rules): proposals, scripts, captions and replies respect it.
The product has no list of its own.
