# YouTube

The channel's numbers correct these rules: what a published video shows wins over them, and what the numbers disprove is removed from
here. Active when YouTube is among the creator's `canali` in the profile.

## What it is for

Depth: whoever comes from a short video and wants to see everything. One video per result, by default 8-20 minutes, 16:9 (the creator's profile can set its own). The long video is the
master: the short ones for TikTok are extracted from it, not the other way round.

## Before shooting

- **Title and cover are decided before the outline.** Together they make a single promise; the video keeps it in the first thirty
  seconds. If the title cannot be written, the video does not exist yet.
- **The outline is in three movements** (`contenuto.md`), with generous times: the dead end is shown before the solution, because it is
  what teaches.
- **Shoot already thinking of the shorts**: every block has a sentence that stands alone as a hook.

## The video

- **The first thirty seconds**: the result or the tension, on screen and aloud; who they are and the context after, in one sentence.
- **One block, one thing**: every block is a chapter with a title that says what is learned.
- **Something changes on screen every few seconds**: screen, face, diagram, object in hand. The stretches of pure talking to camera
  are short.
- **Nothing on screen before the voice says it** and background never still apply here too.
- **Subtitles**: not burned into the video; the subtitles file is uploaded. The on-screen title only where needed.
- **Rhythm**: the cuts remove the gaps but let it breathe; it is not the rhythm of a short.
- **The close** points to another precise video, not to «follow me»: an exception of the platform to «The close» of `contenuto.md`.

## Around

- Description: two lines on what is seen, the chapters with the minutes, the pointers to code and measurements.
- The cover: the subject and three or four words at most; it reads when small.

## From a live recording

The whole recording is uploaded as it was made, horizontal, removing only the long dead times (waits, technical problems), with the
chapters and a title that says what happens. What was said wrong in the live is flagged in the description or cut: it is decided with
the creator.

## Shorts from a long one

They are chosen by words, as from a live recording; each stands alone, without «as I said before». Vertical, with the shorts' frames
and the word-by-word subtitles.
