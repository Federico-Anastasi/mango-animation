# Animations

Defects of the scenes that no check sees. What the engine or `controlla` already prevent or count is not repeated here. A new defect is
added once it has been understood and solved, as a general rule (defect, cause, remedy) without the name of the video; the name and
the story go in the creator's `storia.md` (`docs/PROFILO.md`).

- **3D that flickers**: no stroke recomputed at every frame and no faces on the same plane; the difference between consecutive
  frames is measured, it is not judged by eye.
- **A clip taken from a commercial production**: not everything on a free media site is free. Of every photo or clip the license is
  read on the file's page, and written next to it.
